## Shared by Michele in the 2 Nov 2026 session (from luganoconventions.com)

Pasted into the session as images; source pages to be linked when the network allows
(`luganoconventions.com` is blocked in the build environment as of 2 Nov 2026).

1. **Aerial, from the lake side.** The Palazzo's white stepped roofs; Villa Ciani (pink, hip roof,
   lantern) immediately north-west of it, joined by the low corridor (`Corridoio Villa Ciani` on the
   ground plan); Parco Ciani and the lake beyond; Piazza Indipendenza forecourt with round planters
   and a round canopy kiosk at the main entrance side. Sala A's drum reads as the fan-shaped stepped
   block on the left.
2. **Sala A, conference in progress, from the stage-left side.** Full house; the folded concrete
   ceiling beams (deep V-coffers with downlights and lighting trusses); the balcony sweeping round
   with a concrete front; blue uplights on board-marked concrete walls; white lectern, flowers,
   large projection screen stage right.
3. **Sala A, empty, from the stalls towards the balcony.** Blue upholstered seats on grey frames
   with writing tablets; the balcony slab cutting across; a stair with a blue-topped concrete
   parapet climbing beside the side seating block; grey floor; small windows high up under the
   ceiling.
4. **Sala A, from the balcony to the stage.** Stage with red velvet side curtains and a white screen
   (LVGA logo); stage steps left and right; flat floor in front of the stage then raked rows in a
   fan; the balcony's blue rail running along a faceted concrete parapet in the foreground.
5. **Atrio / Foyer principale during a coffee break.** Crowd of delegates and buffet tables; square
   grey stone columns; light stone floor; the main stair up to the first floor rising centre-back;
   a blue-clad element (lift shaft) at the back right; exhibitor booths along the left; pale timber
   or stone wall panels.

### Lugano Conventions photo galleries

Source pages (all `https://www.luganoconventions.com/it/strutture/palazzo-congressi…`): main page
(`palazzo-congressi`), `/atri-foyer`, `/sala-a`, `/sala-b`, `/sala-c`, `/sala-d`, `/sala-e`. Credit
as stated on every gallery image: **"Credits: Eventi e congressi, Città di Lugano"**. No licence
stated (site footer: © Città di Lugano, Eventi e congressi), so **link only**. Most interior
photos come from a single January 2020 shoot (`IGORG_200125_*`). The image URLs below are the full-size originals
(the gallery serves resized `styles/slick` copies; several originals are 6720 px wide).

Useful text from the room pages, for the game: Sala B is 830 m², 38 × 22 m, splits into
B1 (330 m², 15 × 22) plus B2 and B3 with soundproof folding walls; Atrio B is 199 m²; Sala B is linked
to an office and a goods lift down to the outside forecourt. Sala C is 18 × 6 m, C1 54 m², C2 27,
C3 35, Atrio C 8 × 7 m (54 m²), with a view over Parco Ciani and the lake. Sala D is a small
first-floor "salottino" with its own door. Sala E is on the ground floor, with its own entrance
and a side window wall onto Corso Elvezia / Piazza Indipendenza. Sala A has a 17.5 × 8.7 m stage, a
12 × 4 m movable forestage (heights 0 / 30 / 60 / 100 cm), 9 dressing rooms, and a goods lift
from the forecourt to the stage. The atrium page lists the wardrobe, the pre-sale office/cashier,
the bar/lounge, the internal link to Villa Ciani, and the Ristorante Ciani terrace. Sala G (80 m²,
basement −1, separate entrance) has been the city's "#luganomycity" TV studio since 2023, so it
is out of scope.

### Decisions from Michele's review, 2 Nov 2026

Answers to the review questions, as given:

- **Sala B ceiling is concrete.** There may be some timber parts, but it is mostly concrete. Model
  it as board-marked concrete folded plates with warm light in the folds.
- **The slatted strip high in Sala B** is probably not walkable, and no robot goes up there.
  Treat it as cladding.
