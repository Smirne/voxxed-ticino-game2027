## Shared by Michele in the 2 Nov 2026 session (from luganoconventions.com)

Pasted into the session as images; source pages to be linked when the network allows
(`luganoconventions.com` is blocked in the build environment as of 2 Nov 2026).

1. **Aerial, from the lake side.** The Palazzo's white stepped roofs; Villa Ciani (pink, hip roof,
   lantern) immediately north-west of it, joined by the low corridor (`Corridoio Villa Ciani` on the
   ground plan); Parco Ciani and the lake beyond; Piazza Indipendenza forecourt with round planters
   and a round canopy kiosk at the main entrance side. Sala A's drum reads as the fan-shaped stepped
   block on the left.
2. **Sala A, conference in progress, from the stage-left side.** Full house; the folded concrete
   ceiling beams (deep V-coffers with downlights and lighting trusses); the balcony sweeping round
   with a concrete front; blue uplights on board-marked concrete walls; white lectern, flowers,
   large projection screen stage right.
3. **Sala A, empty, from the stalls towards the balcony.** Blue upholstered seats on grey frames
   with writing tablets; the balcony slab cutting across; a stair with a blue-topped concrete
   parapet climbing beside the side seating block; grey floor; small windows high up under the
   ceiling.
4. **Sala A, from the balcony to the stage.** Stage with red velvet side curtains and a white screen
   (LVGA logo); stage steps left and right; flat floor in front of the stage then raked rows in a
   fan; the balcony's blue rail running along a faceted concrete parapet in the foreground.
5. **Atrio / Foyer principale during a coffee break.** Crowd of delegates and buffet tables; square
   grey stone columns; light stone floor; the main stair up to the first floor rising centre-back;
   a blue-clad element (lift shaft) at the back right; exhibitor booths along the left; pale timber
   or stone wall panels.
