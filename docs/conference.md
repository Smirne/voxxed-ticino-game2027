# Voxxed Days Ticino 2027 — what the conference says about itself

Read from https://vdt27.voxxeddays.ch/ (home and speakers pages) on 11 Nov 2026. Facts first, then
what the game can use. The site changes as the line-up fills: re-read it before shipping.

## Facts

- **Friday 19 February 2027**, Palazzo dei Congressi, Lugano: *"our home for the 12th time"*.
  **Hands-on workshops the day before** (Thursday 18 Feb).
- 12th edition · ~450 attendees · 50+ speakers · 9 tracks.
- Theme line: **"From Developer to Builder"**. Tagline: **"Inspire. Have Fun. Repeat."**
  Ticker: **"Build, Agents + Debug"**.
- Tracks: Agentic Engineering & Tooling · Architecture & Modernization · Cloud Native, Data &
  Infrastructure · Development Practices · Frontend Development · Java Language & Platform · **Mind
  the Geek** (*"robotics, IoT, … creative tech, AI, new toys"*) · People, Practices & Career ·
  Security, Trust & Compliance.
- Tickets: I love VDT CHF 150, Blind Bird 185, Early Bird 215, Student 99, Standard 250.
- Organiser: Exteso Sagl, Lugano. Part of the Voxxed Days Switzerland network (Zürich, CERN).
- Speakers announced so far: Tom Cools (Timefold), Abdellfetah Sghiouar (Google Cloud), Anton
  Arhipov (JetBrains), Loïc Magnette (Oniryx), Christian Schabetsberger (Finxit IT Solutions).
- Copy worth noting: *"the friendliest developer crowd south of the Alps"*, *"Palm trees
  included."*, *"10 minutes on foot from Lugano FFS station, with the lake right around the
  corner"*, *"between Milan and Zürich"*, *"Questions, ideas or just want to say ciao?"*. The site
  has a fake terminal: `$ git log --oneline voxxed/ticino` with `(HEAD → 2027) Build, Agents +
  Debug` and `0000001 initial commit: the very first edition`.

## What the game can use

- **The story's night is real.** "The night before" is the evening after the workshop day: the
  Palazzo has already had people in it all day, the workshop tables are still out, the speakers'
  dinner is that night. It fits Federico and Tiziano sleeping late.
- **The ticker as the run sheet.** *Build, Agents + Debug* is three verbs for three robots, and
  the site's own `git log` look is a ready-made style for the run-sheet menu: each job a commit,
  the finale `(HEAD → 2027)`.
- **"Palm trees included."** The organisers' own joke, so it is safe to reuse: a potted palm the
  robots keep knocking over, or the WiFi password.
- **Mind the Geek** is the track this game and its talk belong in (robotics, creative tech, AI).
- **Real speakers stay out of the game** unless they say yes, like Federico and Tiziano (D5).
  Speaker *roles* (the missing keynote speaker) are fine as generic characters.
